# BIY Intelligence Platform
### GTA Dental Market Intelligence · Build In York

A live intelligence platform mapping **1,400+ dental practices** across the
Greater Toronto Area with competitive analysis, development pipeline overlays,
zoning data, and 10-year growth forecasting.

## Live Platform
👉 **[Open Platform](https://buildinyork.github.io/BIY-Intelligence/)**

## What It Shows
- 📍 Every verified dental practice — mapped, tagged by specialty, flag, and language
- 🏗 Residential development applications with incoming population estimates
- ⚖️ Zoning intelligence — medical/dental permitted uses and parking requirements
- 📈 10-year growth forecast by corridor
- 🏘 Shining Hill Secondary Plan (OPA 48, approved Feb 2026)
- 🚆 GO Transit expansion corridors
- 👥 Population density and household income choropleth layers

## Coverage
York Region (Newmarket, Richmond Hill, Vaughan, Aurora, Markham) ·
Durham Region · Peel Region · Halton Region · Simcoe County

## Usage
Open in any modern browser. Requires internet connection for map tiles and
automatic geocoding of new practices (first load only — results cached locally).

## Data Sources
- Town of Newmarket Development Dashboard (Aug 2026)
- HubSpot CRM — verified practice data (Newmarket + Richmond Hill)
- York Region Official Plan 2022
- Metrolinx GO Expansion Program
- Statistics Canada Census 2021
- Town of Newmarket OPA 48 / Shining Hill Secondary Plan (Feb 2026)
- Town of Newmarket By-law 2017-05 (Urban Centres parking standards)
